My name is Floris den Hengst and I am an Assistant Professor on data science and AI for the human movement sciences.
I have a particular interest in reinforcement learning in human contexts.
 
I am interested in (mutual) adaptivity v.s. control in learning systems, sequential
decision-making problems, goal and constraint specification and uncertainty
quantification. My research touches on practical and fundamental aspects of
explainability and safety of AI.

For my PhD and postdoc, I did research in the [Quantitative Data
Analytics](https://www.qda.cs.vu.nl) and [Learning &
Reasoning](https://lr.cs.vu.nl/) groups of the Vrije Universiteit Amsterdam,  
ING's [AI for FinTech Research](https://se.ewi.tudelft.nl/ai4fintech/), and the [Hybrid Intelligence Center].
I have hands-on experience as a software engineer and data scientist.

* I am part of the [Hybrid Intelligence Center](https://www.hybrid-intelligence-centre.nl/)
* I am a member of the [Network Institute](https://networkinstitute.org/)
* I am a member of [Amsterdam AI](https://amsterdamai.com/en)
* I am a member of the [Amsterdam Institute for Sport Science](Amsterdam Institute for Sport Sience)
* I am (senior) PC member for various journals and conferences, including AAAI, ICML (2026 top-25%), NeurIPS, ICML, WWW, ECAI, ICAPS, ACL.
